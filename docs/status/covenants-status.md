# Kaspa L1 Covenants Status

This document outlines the current support boundary for Kaspa L1 Covenants (KIP-17/KIP-20) in HardKAS.

## Supported
- ✅ **1:1 auth-bound covenants through the CLI**: `hardkas silver covenant genesis` creates a covenant and `hardkas silver covenant transition` advances it, as transaction v1 against the canonical localnet or another simnet node (`--url`, or the one the workspace declares), which proves its identity by what it reports before any key is used (`toccata.covenant.auth-1to1-transition.v1`, evidenced on real simnet nodes: the canonical localnet and a node of the caller's own through `pnpm gauntlet:toccata --url`). `testnet-<n>` is accepted behind the payments' opt-in and is not evidenced on testnet-10; mainnet is refused. `hardkas silver doctor` reports whether the toolchains and the node those commands would use (`--url`/`--network`, else the workspace's endpoint) are ready.
- ✅ **Covenant Artifact Generation**: builders can architect covenants and inspect properties structurally (`hardkas.covenants.buildCovenant()`, legacy).
- ✅ **Transaction v1 signing**: `hardkas.tx.sign()` signs a v1 plan for a node network with the managed kaspa-wasm, which signs v1.
- ✅ **One v1 engine for covenants**: `hardkas silver covenant genesis` and `covenant transition` plan, check, sign and send with the SDK's engine (`hardkas.covenants`), as `hardkas silver engine` and the SDK do. `hardkas silver deploy` and `spend` keep sending transaction v0 by default (the certified path) and use the engine only with `--tx-version 1`. Normal payments, batches and consolidations stay transaction v0.
- ✅ **Covenant history and lineage from the workspace's records**: `hardkas.covenants.history()`, `lineage()`, `inspect()` and `getState()` (and `hardkas silver covenant inspect`) follow a covenant through this workspace's own records and the node's unspent outputs at its addresses. The genesis is anchored by recomputing its covenant id (consensus `covenant_id`), transitions are linked by the outpoints they spend, and each live output's declared state is checked against its address. The node has no query by covenant id: a transaction this workspace did not record is not seen, only its effect (an output the lineage expects unspent is not listed).

## Not supported by the SDK
- ❌ **Covenant Deploy / Spend Plans (script/witness forms)**: `hardkas.covenants.planDeploy()` and `planSpend()` refuse with `COVENANT_PLAN_UNSUPPORTED` and never substitute an ordinary payment; the v1 engine plans covenants with `planGenesis()`, `planTransition()`, `planContractDeploy()` and `planContractSpend()`.
- ❌ **Covenant queries by id at the node**: there is no RPC that lists UTXOs by `covenantId`; HardKAS does not invent one (see the lineage above).
- ❌ **v1 plans in the SDK's simulated mode**: `hardkas.tx.sign()` refuses them with `TX_V1_SIMULATION_UNSUPPORTED`. The separate, experimental `@hardkas/simulator` package does validate transaction-v1 covenant bindings (genesis ids recomputed, rules aligned with rusty-kaspa v2.1.0 in the 2026-11-06 repair). It is an approximate model, not covenant evidence: only a node's answer counts ([limitations](./limitations.md#the-simulator-is-an-approximate-model)).
- ⚠️ **Topologies other than 1:1 (the SDK's engine; simnet evidence only)**: the SDK's v1 engine (`hardkas.covenants.planGenesis` / `planTransition`, `hardkas silver engine`) plans 1:N, N:M and terminating transitions, and `packages/sdk/test/covenant-topologies-node.simnet.test.ts` sends them to a real rusty-kaspad v2.1.0 on simnet (the harness's own node). Accepted and mined, with the covenant id the engine computed: a 1:N auth fanout (1 → 3), an N:M cov leader + delegate (2 → 2), the signed KCC-20 example transfer (2 → 2, two signers) and a singleton that ends (1 → 0). Every invalid variant the engine refuses (too many continuations or inputs, an output not holding its state, another covenant id, an unbound continuation, the leader out of place, a slot signed by another key) the node refuses too. What a policy computes (a `require`, amount conservation, minting) the engine does not evaluate: those variants are signed, and the node refuses them. Observed: the node accepts zero continuations of the fanout, which declares no termination (the engine warns that the policy decides). Not evidenced on testnet-10, not a corpus capability (`generalCovenantSupport` stays `NOT_CLAIMED`), and `hardkas silver covenant genesis` and `hardkas silver covenant transition` stay 1:1.

`hardkas.covenants.isSupported()` answers whether covenant transactions can be built and submitted here: the managed silverc resolves, the managed kaspa-wasm is verified and signs v1, and the canonical node proves its identity (the checks `hardkas silver doctor` reports; `hardkas silver doctor --url <url>` and `hardkas capabilities --url <url>` check another node by what it reports).

## Builder Lab Guardrails
We adhere strictly to the principle of "No Simulated Product Labs" or "Smoke and Mirrors".

We will not build simulated Covenant Vaults or products that falsely claim to execute covenant logic when the underlying protocol layers do not yet support it.
